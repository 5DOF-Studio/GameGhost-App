## HUD Reading Rules (CoD MW3 — Model Instructions)

### Kill Feed
- **Yellow text = player's own kill** — unmistakable confirmation
- **Blue name (left) killed Red name (right)** = teammate killed enemy
- **Red name (left) killed Blue name (right)** = enemy killed teammate
- **Blue killed Blue** = accidental teamkill (friendly fire, HC mode)
- Kill feed shows ALL map engagements, not just the player's
- Accolades trail AFTER a kill — don't count accolade popups as separate kills

### Accolades & Medals
- Accolades appear as trailing text after a kill (LONGSHOT, ONE SHOT ONE KILL, DEFENSE, HFA)
- +XP values accumulate with each accolade — don't treat XP popup as a new event
- LONGSHOT includes distance (e.g., 59.88M) — useful for positioning context

### Hit Markers
- **Red hit markers** = damage dealt (lethal/weapon impact)
- **White hit markers** = non-lethal impact confirmed (flash, stun, concussion)
- White markers after a throw = **intel gathered, not damage** — player now knows enemy position

### Equipment Notation Convention
```
tac:FLASH / tac:STUN / tac:CONCUSSION    — tactical (non-lethal) equipment
lethal:FRAG / lethal:SEMTEX / lethal:MINE — lethal equipment
```

### Equipment Hit Convention
```
hit:WHITE                        — non-lethal confirmed impact
hit:WHITE(resist:BATTLE_HARDENED) — enemy has tactical resistance perk (helmet+blast icon)
hit:RED                          — damage dealt
```
- `resist:BATTLE_HARDENED` = helmet+blast icon on hit = enemy resists tactical effects
- Tactical read after hit: "enemy confirmed at location, resistance level known, decide: push/peek/wait"

### Assist & Tactical Payoff Chain
- A tactical hit (flash/stun) **tags** the enemy in the game's assist system
- When a teammate kills a tagged enemy, the tagger earns **assist XP** (~25 XP)
- Low XP on a teammate's kill = assist credit, not a direct kill
- This reveals **coordinated team play**: flash→intel→teammate push→kill→assist
- Narration should connect the chain: "Wakandan's flash called it out, teammate finished the job"
- Look for the payoff: if a flash hit appears in segment N, check segments N+1 through N+3 for teammate kills at that location

### Compass & Location
- Compass at top of HUD shows bearing (0-360) + named callout (ALPHA_GATE, FORKLIFT, CONTAINERS, etc.)
- Use `facing:DIR(bearing) at:CALLOUT` format (e.g., `facing:E(104) at:FORKLIFT`)
- Arrow notation for movement: `facing:E→NE at:FORKLIFT→CONTAINERS`

### Player Status Icons
- Skull icon on downed body = confirmed dead (not revivable in current state)
- Blue cross on downed body = downed teammate (revivable)
- "YOU" tag = the player being watched (in killcam/replay)

### Scoreboard
- OPFOR vs Task Force 141 (or other faction names)
- Score/Kills/Deaths per player
- Round score shown as large numbers (e.g., blue 1 / red 0)

### General Rules
- HUD is LIMITED in Hardcore — no minimap, reduced indicators
- Friendly fire is ON — teamkills happen
- "left the game" in feed = ragequit (player count drops)
- FINAL KILL replay shows the last kill of the round from the killer's POV
- Bomb status alerts: "ENEMY HAS THE BOMB!" = objective callout
